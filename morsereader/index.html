Morse Reader

Decode Morse code from your radio's audio, and send it back. Morse Reader finds the tone's pitch and learns the sending speed on its own, copes with room echo and noise, and runs entirely on your iPhone or iPad, offline.

  • Any pitch from 400 to 1200 Hz, any speed from 5 to 40 WPM
  • A live spectrum to pick one station when two are on the air
  • Send messages through a cable or the speaker, with automatic callsign ID
