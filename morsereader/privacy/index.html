Morse Reader Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

In short: Morse Reader doesn't collect, store or share any of your data. Audio is decoded on your device as it arrives, and the app never connects to the internet.

Microphone and audio

Morse Reader uses the microphone (or an audio cable plugged into your device) only while you're listening, to decode Morse code. The audio is processed on your device in real time. It isn't sent anywhere.

So that you can save a recording of something that didn't decode well, the app keeps the most recent 60 seconds of audio in memory, where newer audio replaces it and it's gone when the app closes. It's only written to a file if you choose Save last 60 seconds of audio in Settings, and that file only goes where you send it from the share sheet.

Decoded text and messages

Decoded text stays on your device. It leaves only if you share it yourself. Messages you write to send as Morse are played out of your device's speaker or audio jack; the draft is kept on your device so it's there next time.

Settings

Your settings, including a callsign if you enter one, are stored on your device in the app's own storage. They aren't sent anywhere.

What the app doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking
  • No third-party code that collects data
  • No network connections of any kind

Children

Morse Reader doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
